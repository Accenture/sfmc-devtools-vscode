## mcdev-log TextMate grammar

The embedded `mcdev-log` grammar (`syntaxes/mcdev-log.tmLanguage`) and its language
configuration (`mcdev-log.configuration.json`) are adapted from IBM's
[vscode-log-output-colorizer](https://github.com/IBM/vscode-log-output-colorizer)
grammar (`log.tmLanguage` / `log.configuration.json`). The original files were
pruned and re-tuned for the DevTools "mcdev" Output channel.
